Welcome — Module 3: Clinic Partner (CCS) & Clinic Manager

This module is designed for Clinic Partners (CCS) and Clinic Managers. Your role is not day-to-day operational — it is governance, accountability, approval, and escalation. This module explains what that means in practice under the Final SOP.

🎯
By the end of this module you will be able to:
  • Understand your accountability for AR as a clinic-level KPI
  • Apply the monitoring and review cadence for your clinic
  • Execute the escalation pathway for high-risk and unrecoverable debt
  • Apply the correct approval thresholds for Doubtful Debt Write-Offs
  • Ensure delegation is in place when your Practice Manager is on leave

SECTION 1

Your Accountability — It Starts Here

🛡️
Responsibility for invoicing, accounts receivable, and debt management sits at the clinic level — with the Practice Manager and with you. The SOP is explicit about this.
📊 It Is a KPI
AR and debt management is a key performance indicator for your clinic. Results are reported to Regional Partners and leadership monthly. Poor performance is visible and tracked.
💸 Revenue Impact
Write-offs and invoice adjustments from mismanaged debt directly reduce reported revenue. This impacts your clinic's P&L, EBITDA, and resulting dividends. The connection is direct.
👥 Your Team Owns the Process
You are accountable for ensuring your Practice Manager and admin team execute the SOP consistently. If the PM is on leave — you ensure delegation is formally arranged before they go.
⬆️ Escalation Is Yours
When unrecoverable debt is identified, escalation to the Regional Partner comes through you. You are also the first approval checkpoint in the Doubtful Debt Write-Off process.
SECTION 2

Your Monitoring & Review Responsibilities

Your oversight is structured across three cadences. Each has defined actions and ownership at clinic level.

📅 Weekly
  • Review Ageing Debts Report — all buckets
  • Confirm PM and admin are executing follow-up actions
  • Flag any 60+ or 90+ day movements
  • Tier 3 clinics: participate in RP weekly check-in call
📅 Monthly
  • Review Doubtful Debtors XLSX prepared by PM
  • Provide your approval before forwarding to Regional Partner
  • Participate in RP monthly performance review
  • Review write-offs approved in the period
⚡ Immediate
  • Any unrecoverable debt identified → escalate to RP immediately
  • 90+ day debt increase → notify RP same day
  • PM on leave without delegation → arrange before leave begins
💡
Do not wait for the scheduled monthly review to escalate. Any 90+ day increase or unrecoverable debt identified must be escalated immediately — ad hoc escalation is an expected part of the process.

Your Write-Off Approval Role

The Practice Manager prepares the Doubtful Debtors XLSX and submits it to you first. You review and approve — then forward to the Regional Partner. The approval thresholds then determine how far up it goes:

Regional Partner
Under $1,000RP can approve after Clinic Partner sign-off
General Manager
$1,000 – $4,999GM approval required
CFO
$5,000+CFO approval required
⚠️
Important: Approval must be obtained from all Partners before any write-off is processed in Nookal. Do not instruct the admin team to process a write-off until the correct approval is confirmed. Write-offs directly reduce P&L, EBITDA, and dividends.

Write-Off Criteria

  • •Invoice overdue by 90–120+ days from unresponsive debtor despite repeated recovery efforts
  • •Debt is unlikely to be recovered via a collection agency
  • •Third Party Payer has determined the invoice does not meet claim requirements
  • •The clinic must be profitable for write-offs to be approved
