SECTION 1

Why Debt Management Matters — For All of Us

⚠️
Unpaid invoices = lost revenue. Every dollar of unrecovered debt directly impacts the viability of the clinic and the entire Healthia network.
💰 Cash Flow Impact
The clinic needs cash to operate. Unpaid invoices delay payments to suppliers, staff, and overheads. Collecting payment at the right time keeps the clinic running.
🏆 Your Role Matters
Admin is the first line of defence. How you collect payment and follow up directly shapes the result. You are essential to this process.
📊 It's a KPI
Accounts receivable and debt management is a key performance indicator for each clinic. Results are reported to Clinic Partners and Regional Partners monthly.
🚫 Nil Debt Over 90 Days
The network target is nil debt over 90 days. Minimal debt should be over 60 days. Do not leave ageing debt unactioned.

SECTION 3

Invoicing & Payment Collection at Time of Service

Your responsibilities — every appointment, every time:

1

Generate Invoice

Create Private & Third Party invoices immediately at time-of-service delivery. Never delay.

2

Submit via HICAPS / Tyro

Use HICAPS Digital or Tyro Health first. If unavailable, email the invoice through Nookal at time of service.

3

Collect Payment

Private clients pay on the spot. Offer: in person, phone card payment, or bank transfer (invoice number as reference).

4

Client Can't Pay?

Print invoice. Explain options. Place OUTSTANDING DEBT alert on Nookal profile. Set 48hr follow-up reminder.

📌
Three payment methods to always offer: (1) In person at reception  (2) Debit or credit card over the phone  (3) Bank transfer using the invoice number as a reference
SECTION 4

Daily Bank Account Reconciliation — 5 Steps

📅
This must be completed every business day. If you are the primary person and going on leave — formally delegate before you go. Debt management does not pause.

There should be a primary nominated person (Practice Manager or Senior Admin) but it is best to have multiple team members involved so that remittances are receipted in a timely manner and the bank account is reconciled daily.

  • 1
    Step 1 — Download Bank Transactions
    Log in to the clinic bank account and download all transactions for the day.
  • 2
    Step 2 — Reconcile, Remittances & Receipting
    Check HICAPS/EFTPOS deposits against previous end-of-day settlement slips. Check other deposits against remittances received via email, post, or Nookal portals. Receipt payments in Nookal — date as the day receipted, not the bank date. If delayed, add a note: "received in bank [date]".
    Tip: highlight or delete reconciled transactions on the spreadsheet as you go so you can track progress.
  • 3
    Step 3 — Adjustments
    If invoice amounts differ, follow the Nookal Invoice Adjustments Guide. Do not adjust without following the correct process.
  • 4
    Step 4 — Unreconciled Transactions & Inter-Clinic Transfers
    For any deposits that don't belong to your clinic, complete an Inter-Clinic Transfer. If you cannot identify a deposit, contact Finance/Operations Business Partner and request the Inter-Clinic Transfer Form.
  • 5
    Step 5 — Run Nookal Banking Report
    Run the Banking Report and confirm bank transfer totals match Nookal. For Medicare/DVA/NDIS clinics: the combined total of Bank Transfer + NDIS Funded + Public Health + DVA should match the bank.
💡
File format tip: Export reports as XLSX — not CSV. CSV files do not save notes you add after export. The XLSX file has 2 tabs: Parameters and Details. Work from the Details tab.
SECTION 5

When a Client Leaves Without Paying

🚨
Act immediately — do not wait for their next appointment. Clients who owe money may cancel to avoid paying.
🟢 Right Now
  • Print invoice and give or email to client
  • Explain the three payment options
  • Place OUTSTANDING DEBT alert on their Nookal profile
  • Set 48-hour follow-up reminder in Nookal
🟠 Within 48 Hours
  • Call the client — leave voicemail if no answer
  • Send SMS follow-up via Nookal
  • Email 0–30 Day template with invoice attached
  • Do NOT wait for the next appointment
🔵 Before Next Appointment
  • Check if payment has been received
  • If still unpaid — call again before the appointment
  • Alert team: client may cancel to avoid paying
  • Consider whether to proceed with the appointment
📌
The OUTSTANDING DEBT alert in Nookal is visible to all admin team members. It ensures no one books the client or lets them leave without first checking their balance.

SECTION 7

Nookal Ageing Debts Report — How to Run It

How to Generate the Report

  • ✓Go to the Reports tab in Nookal
  • ✓Select "Ageing Debts"
  • ✓Set Date to "10 Years" — always
  • ✓Select all Providers (or filter by provider)
  • ✓Select all Payers (or filter by type)
  • ✓Tick "Show Expanded" — one row per invoice
  • ✓Include Services, Classes, Inventory, Passes, Other
  • ✓Export as XLSX — NOT CSV

Key Filter Settings

FilterWhat to do
DateAlways use "10 Years" for accurate data
LocationFilter by site if multiple locations
Show ExpandedTICK THIS — one row per invoice
SortBy Client or Type to group logically
All typesInclude all: Services, Classes, Inventory, Passes, Other
💡
Pro tips: Add notes to your XLSX to track follow-up actions — CSV won't save your notes. Click the invoice number in Nookal to open, process, re-send, or check the online portal directly from the report.
