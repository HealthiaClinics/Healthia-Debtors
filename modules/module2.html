SECTION 1

Multi-Disciplinary Clinics — Podiatry Debt Responsibility

📌
This rule applies to every multi-disciplinary Healthia clinic that co-locates with Healthia Podiatry. Read it carefully — it is a defined responsibility.
In any multi-disciplinary clinic where Healthia Podiatry operates:

It is the responsibility of the physiotherapy clinic's Practice Manager and admin team to chase and manage outstanding debts for Podiatry as well as Physiotherapy.

Podiatry debt does not manage itself and does not belong to a separate team — it is your responsibility as the co-located physio clinic to ensure all Podiatry ageing debtors in your clinic are followed up in line with the same SOP procedures and timelines.
✅ What This Means in Practice
  • Run the Ageing Debts Report across all providers — including Podiatry
  • Follow up Podiatry private debtors within 48 hours, same as Physio
  • Apply the OUTSTANDING DEBT alert to Podiatry patient profiles
  • Include Podiatry debtors in your weekly and monthly reporting to your Clinic Partner and RP
  • Submit Podiatry doubtful debtors as part of the same write-off procedure
💡
When generating your Ageing Debts Report in Nookal — select all Providers, not just Physio. This ensures Podiatry debtors appear in your report and are not overlooked.
SECTION 2

The Ageing Debt Follow-Up Framework

Ageing debt management is a structured, step-by-step process. Each ageing bucket has a defined set of actions. Your job is to work through each bucket systematically — never skip a step and never let debt age without documented follow-up.

0–30 Days
Standard

All Payer Types

  • Check for rejected claims via HICAPS / Nookal
  • Resubmit claims promptly

Private Clients

  • Call + SMS + email within 48 hours
  • OUTSTANDING DEBT alert on profile
  • Set Nookal reminder
31–60 Days
Elevated

Private

  • Call + take payment over phone
  • Further Reminder email + invoice
  • Private note on invoice
  • Confirm OUTSTANDING DEBT alert

Third Party

  • Check portals and platform status
  • Call Third Party — confirm receipt and timeframe
  • Follow-up email + invoice
  • Private note on invoice
61–90 Days
Final Notice

Private

  • Call + FINAL NOTICE email + invoice
  • Private note on invoice
  • Confirm OUTSTANDING DEBT alert

Third Party

  • Call — ask about delay, flag client outcomes impact
  • FINAL NOTICE email + invoice
  • OUTSTANDING THIRD PARTY DEBT alert
  • Private note on invoice
90+ Days
Escalate

All Types — Determine Recoverability

  • If recoverable: repeat Step 3 actions — call, Final Notice, increase urgency
  • If unrecoverable: escalate to Clinic Partner/Team Leader → initiate Doubtful Debt Write-Off Procedure (Step 5)
💡
Run the Ageing Debts Report weekly. Work through each bucket in order — always Private and Third Party separately. Document every action with a private note and a Nookal reminder.
SECTION 3

Steps 1 & 2 — 0 to 60 Day Ageing Debtors

🟢 Step 1 — 0 to 30 Day Ageing Debtors

Check Rejections (All Payer Types)

  • ✓Check HICAPS Report for rejected claims
  • ✓Review online payment portal websites
  • ✓Check Ageing Debts Report for rejections
  • ✓Resubmit via HICAPS Digital / Tyro or email immediately
  • !Rejected claim may fall to the client — act immediately
  • !Avoid multiple unpaid services accumulating for the same client

Private Clients — 48-Hour Follow-Up

  • ✓Call client — leave voicemail if no answer
  • ✓Send SMS follow-up via Nookal
  • ✓Email 0–30 Day Reminder template with invoice attached
  • ✓Place OUTSTANDING DEBT alert on Nookal profile
  • ✓Set Nookal reminder for follow-up before next appointment
  • !Do NOT wait for the next appointment to chase
🟡 Step 2 — 31 to 60 Day Ageing Debtors

Private Ageing Debtors — 31 to 60 Days

  • ✓Call client or entity — take payment over the phone
  • ✓Send Further Reminder email + invoice (email or SMS)
  • ✓Add Private Note on invoice with all follow-up actions
  • ✓Confirm OUTSTANDING DEBT alert is on client file

Third Party Payers — 31 to 60 Days

  • ✓Review claim details on digital platforms / portals
  • ✓Call Third Party — confirm invoice received & payment timeframe
  • ✓Send follow-up email with invoice to Third Party Payer
  • ✓Add Private Note on invoice with follow-up actions
📝
After every contact attempt — add a Private Note to the invoice in Nookal. Record: date, method (call/email/SMS), outcome, and next planned action.
SECTION 4

Steps 3, 4 & 5 — 61+ Days and Unrecoverable Debt

🔴 Step 3 — 61 to 90 Day Ageing Debtors

Private — 61 to 90 Days

  • ✓Call + send Final Notice email + invoice
  • ✓Add Private Note on invoice
  • ✓Confirm OUTSTANDING DEBT alert is on file

Third Party — 61 to 90 Days

  • ✓Call Third Party — ask about reason for delay, confirm nothing is outstanding from the clinic
  • ✓Flag: client treatment may be impacted by outstanding invoices affecting client outcomes
  • ✓Send Final Notice email + invoice to Third Party Payer
  • ✓Place OUTSTANDING THIRD PARTY DEBT alert on client file
  • ✓Add Private Note on invoice with all actions taken
💡
For Third Party payers at 61–90 days: flagging the impact on client outcomes can be very effective in prompting payment. Be professional and factual — not threatening.
🚨 Step 4 — 90+ Day Ageing Debtors

Determine if debt is recoverable or not.

  • 1Run Ageing Debts Report — identify all 90+ day column debtors
  • 2For each debtor: assess recoverability — has the client/payer been responsive? Any partial payments?
  • 3If recoverable: repeat Step 3 actions — call, Final Notice, increase urgency
  • !If unrecoverable: escalate to Clinic Partner/Team Leader → initiate Step 5
📋 Step 5 — Doubtful Debt Write-Off Procedure

Criteria for write-off: invoices overdue by 90–120+ days from an unresponsive debtor despite repeated recovery efforts, OR a Third Party that has determined the invoice does not meet claim requirements.

  • 1
    Step 1 — Generate Report & Identify Doubtful Debtors
    Download Ageing Debts Report. Identify all debtors in the 90+ day column. Save as: Doubtful Debtors – [COST CODE] – [MONTH].xlsx
    Example: Doubtful Debtors – 1000 – MAY.xlsx
  • 2
    Step 2 — Get Approval
    Send file to Clinic Partner or Team Leader for review and approval. Once approved by Clinic Partner, they forward to Regional Partner. See approval thresholds below.
  • 3
    Step 3 — Write Off in Nookal
    Once approved: Open Invoice → Click on item → Adjust Total → Enter $0 → Reason: "WRITE OFF" → Save

Approval Thresholds

Regional Partner
Under $1,000Regional Partner can approve
General Manager
$1,000 – $4,999General Manager approval required
CFO
$5,000+CFO approval required
📅
Doubtful Debt Write-Offs are submitted monthly to the Regional Partner — not ad hoc. The clinic must be profitable for write-offs to be approved. Approval must be obtained from all Partners before any write-off is processed in Nookal.
SECTION 5

Nookal Invoice Adjustment Rules

🔒
Lock Period = ONE business day. After one business day, backdating and voiding are not permitted. Friday/Saturday/Sunday invoices cannot be backdated or voided after 11:59pm Monday.
ActionAllowed After Lock Period?Notes
Change Payer, Case or Addressee✓ YESWon't change revenue figures
Change Code & Edit Description✓ YESWon't change revenue figures
Change Total (line-item price)✓ YES (not backdated)Click item → Adjust Total → enter new amount → provide reason → Save
Change Payment Method✓ YES (not backdated)Click payment → Change Method
Add services, classes or late items✗ NOWould increase clinic/clinician revenue for a prior period
Void Invoice✗ NOWould decrease clinic/clinician revenue for a prior period
Backdate Invoice or Changes✗ NOWould change clinic/clinician revenue for a prior period
Change Provider✗ NOWould alter clinician revenue and provider activity reports
🎫
If an adjustment is required and cannot be made with the above options, submit a ticket via ASSIST (Category: Operations → Nookal: Invoice Amendments). Include the invoice number and details of what needs to change. If revenue is backdated outside the revenue period, Finance must review.
SECTION 6

Recording Follow-Up Actions in Nookal

⚠️
Every follow-up action must be documented. If it is not recorded in Nookal, it did not happen — and you will have no evidence if escalation or a write-off is needed.
📝 Private Note on Invoice
After every follow-up — add a Private Note on the invoice. Include:
  • Date of contact
  • Method used (call/SMS/email)
  • Outcome of contact
  • Next planned action
🔔 Reminder in Nookal
Set a Nookal follow-up reminder after every contact attempt.

Do NOT rely on memory. The reminder keeps the cycle alive and ensures nothing slips — especially important before the client's next appointment.
🚨 Alert on Client File
Ensure alerts are active and visible to all team members:
  • Private debt: OUTSTANDING DEBT
  • Third Party debt: OUTSTANDING THIRD PARTY DEBT
This prevents other admin booking clients in without checking first.
SECTION 7

Reporting to Clinic Partners & Regional Partners

Regular reporting keeps leadership informed and enables early escalation of high-risk debt. This is part of your formal responsibility.

📅 Weekly
  • Run Ageing Debts Report in Nookal
  • Review all buckets — note movement from prior week
  • Flag any new 60+ day debts to Clinic Partner
  • Tier 3 clinics: attend weekly check-in call with RP
📅 Monthly
  • Prepare Doubtful Debtors XLSX report if applicable
  • Submit to Clinic Partner → Regional Partner for approval
  • Attend or provide data for RP monthly performance review
  • Review write-offs approved in the period
⚡ Immediate — Ad Hoc
  • Unrecoverable debt identified → escalate immediately
  • 90+ day debt increases → notify Clinic Partner same day
  • Unidentified bank deposit → contact Finance/Ops BP
  • Invoice adjustment outside normal rules → ASSIST ticket
