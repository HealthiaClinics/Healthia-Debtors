Quick Reference

Email Templates

The exact wording from the SOP for each ageing bucket. Copy, paste into Nookal, and fill in the bracketed details — don't freelance the wording, especially for the Final Notice.

Reminder (0–30 days) Further Reminder (30–60 days) Final Notice (61–90 & 90+ days)

Reminder

Use for 0–30 day ageing debts
0–30 DAYS
Subject: Reminder - Outstanding Invoice
Dear [Patient's Name], Reminder - Outstanding Invoice We write to advise you that our records indicate the attached invoice is outstanding and now overdue. Payment can be made: • In person by visiting the clinic, • Via debit or credit card over the phone, or • Via bank transfer to the bank account detailed on the invoice (please use invoice number as a reference) Should you be unable to make full payment, please contact the clinic to discuss an alternative payment arrangement. If the attached invoice has already been paid, or if you believe the invoice is not correct, please contact the clinic or email confirmation of those details so the payment can be allocated against the invoice. Thank you for your attention. Best regards, [Clinic Name]

Further Reminder

Use for 30–60 day ageing debts
30–60 DAYS
Subject: Reminder - Outstanding Invoice
Dear [Patient's Name], Reminder - Outstanding Invoice Further to our previous correspondence, the attached invoice remains outstanding and is now further overdue. Payment can be made: • In person by visiting the clinic, • Via debit or credit card over the phone, or • Via bank transfer to the bank account detailed on the invoice (please use invoice number as a reference) Should you be unable to make full payment, please contact the clinic to discuss an alternative payment arrangement. If the attached invoice has already been paid, or if you believe the invoice is not correct, please contact the clinic or email confirmation of those details so the payment can be allocated against the invoice. We appreciate your promptness in addressing this outstanding invoice. Thank you for your attention. Best regards, [Clinic Name]

Final Notice

Use for 61–90 and 90+ day ageing debts
61–90 & 90+ DAYS
Subject: Final Notice - Outstanding Invoice
Dear [Patient's Name], FINAL NOTICE - Outstanding Invoice Further to our previous correspondence, the attached invoice remains outstanding and is now significantly overdue. Payment can be made: • In person by visiting the clinic, • Via debit or credit card over the phone, or • Via bank transfer to the bank account detailed on the invoice (please use invoice number as a reference) Should you be unable to make full payment, please contact the clinic to discuss an alternative payment arrangement. If this invoice remains outstanding, more formal debt recovery steps may be taken, including referral to a debt collection agency. If the attached invoice has already been paid, or if you believe the invoice is not correct, please contact the clinic or email confirmation of those details so the payment can be allocated against the invoice. We appreciate your promptness in addressing this outstanding invoice. Thank you for your attention. Best regards, [Clinic Name]
BEFORE SENDING THIS ONEThis is the last step before a debt may be considered for the Doubtful Debt Write-Off process. Make sure the ageing bucket and client details are correct before sending — this wording references formal debt collection.
